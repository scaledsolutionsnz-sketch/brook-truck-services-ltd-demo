What we work on

Light and heavy transport repairs from the workshop at 307 Flaxton Road, Rangiora — trucks, trailers, buses, motorhomes, utes and machinery, with breakdown and after-hours cover across North Canterbury.

Truck servicing & repairs

Scheduled servicing and repair work on linehaul rigs, tippers, farm trucks and light commercials. Faults get diagnosed properly before parts get thrown at them, and you get told what needs doing now versus what can wait for the next service.

  • Scheduled and pre-COF servicing
  • Engine, cooling and driveline repairs
  • Fault diagnosis on European and Japanese platforms

COF prep & brake testing

There's a brake testing machine here in the workshop, which is the bit most places don't have. That means brakes get tested, adjusted and re-tested on site before your Certificate of Fitness, rather than the truck doing laps of Canterbury chasing a booking.

  • Brake testing on site
  • COF and WOF preparation and repairs
  • Re-check after failed brake items

Trailer & running gear repairs

Trailers, tippers, transporters and the long units nobody else wants in their yard. Suspension, axles, hubs, air lines, brakes and towing gear, all sorted with heavy vehicle equipment rather than improvised on the ground.

  • Suspension, axles and hubs
  • Air brake systems and lines
  • Long and specialist trailers welcome

Welding & fabrication

Bell housings, chassis rails, decks, brackets and mounts. If a component has cracked, bent or worn through, it gets cut out and welded up so it holds, rather than patched to get through the next trip.

  • Structural and chassis welding
  • Bell housing and driveline repairs
  • Brackets, mounts and one-off fabrication

Motorhomes, buses & light commercial

Motorhomes, buses, vans and utes get the heavy vehicle hoist and the heavy vehicle tooling — which is exactly what you want when it's a clutch and flywheel job rather than an oil change. Cars and SUVs are looked after too.

  • Clutch, flywheel and driveline work
  • Brakes, suspension and servicing
  • Cars, SUVs, utes and earthmoving equipment

Auto electrical & heavy-duty batteries

Starting and charging faults tracked down properly. Brook Truck Services is the Battery Town Rangiora commercial partner, so Varta and Endurant heavy-duty batteries for trucks, trailers, buses and fleets are supplied, tested and fitted here with nationwide warranty support.

  • Battery testing and replacement
  • Varta and Endurant heavy-duty batteries
  • Starting and charging fault finding

Breakdown & after-hours service

It's on the sign on Flaxton Road and it has been for years: breakdown and after-hours service. Ring the workshop during the day on 03 313 6361, or the after-hours mobile on 027 5200 711 when it stops outside of opening hours.

  • After-hours mobile: 027 5200 711
  • Roadside and out-of-hours repairs
  • Rangiora and wider North Canterbury
