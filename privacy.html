Privacy Policy

How Brook Truck Services Ltd collects, uses and looks after the information you give us.

1. Who we are

Brook Truck Services Ltd is a light and heavy transport repair workshop at 307 Flaxton Road, Rangiora 7400, New Zealand. This policy explains how we handle personal information, in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

We only collect what we need in order to quote, carry out and invoice work:

  • Your name, business name, phone number and email address.
  • Vehicle, trailer or equipment details, including registration, make, model and service history with us.
  • Job records: what was reported, what was done, parts fitted and inspection results.
  • Billing and payment details needed to invoice you or run an account.

3. How we collect it

Usually directly from you — over the phone, by email, or when you drop a vehicle in. We may also receive details from a fleet manager, insurer or driver acting on your behalf.

4. How we use it

  • To quote, book in and carry out repairs and servicing.
  • To contact you about a job, including approving extra work found during a strip-down.
  • To invoice you and manage payment.
  • To keep service history so future work on the same vehicle is quicker and better informed.
  • To meet record-keeping obligations relating to compliance and inspection work.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only where it's needed to do the job, for example with parts and battery suppliers, certifying inspectors, subcontracted specialists, our accountant, or where the law requires it.

6. This website

This site does not contain any contact or feedback forms. The Call and Email buttons open your own phone dialler or email application, so nothing you type is stored by this website. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, images from a content delivery network, and an embedded Google Map, and those providers may record standard technical information such as your IP address and browser. Following a link to our Google listing, Facebook page or another platform takes you to that provider's own privacy terms.

7. Storage and security

Records are held in our workshop management and accounting systems, with access limited to the people who need it. We take reasonable steps to protect information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

8. How long we keep it

Job and invoice records are kept for as long as we need them for servicing history, warranty, tax and legal purposes, then securely disposed of.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it's wrong. Get in touch and we'll sort it out.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always the one published on this page.

11. Contact us

Brook Truck Services Ltd, 307 Flaxton Road, Rangiora 7400. Phone 03 313 6361, after hours 027 5200 711, or email the workshop.
