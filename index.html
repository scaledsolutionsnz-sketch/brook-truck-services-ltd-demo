About the workshop

A Rangiora workshop set up for the heavy stuff

Brook Truck Services is a light and heavy transport repair workshop on Flaxton Road in Rangiora, working for the transport operators, contractors, farmers and owner-drivers of North Canterbury. Cars, SUVs and utes right through to trucks, trailers, buses, motorhomes and earthmoving equipment.

It's a heavy vehicle workshop rather than a car garage with a big door — including a brake testing machine on site — so Certificate of Fitness work, driveline jobs and the long awkward units all get sorted in the one place.

  • Light and heavy under one roofCars and utes through to trucks, trailers, motorhomes and earthmoving gear.
  • Brake testing machine on siteTest, adjust and re-test in the workshop instead of chasing a slot somewhere else.
  • Battery Town Rangiora commercial partnerVarta and Endurant heavy-duty batteries supplied and fitted, with nationwide warranty support.
